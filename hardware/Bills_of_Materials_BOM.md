# Bill of Materials (BOM)

This document lists the electronic components used in the Respira system, including their technical specifications, quantities and functions.

## Electronic Components

| Component | Specification | Quantity | Function |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Arduino | Arduino Nano / final board to confirm | 1 | Microcontroller for sensor acquisition and motor control |
| Differential pressure sensor | MPXV7002DP | 1 | Measures differential pressure generated by breathing |
| Servo motor | Model to confirm | 1 | Drives the mechanical bubble-forming mechanism |
| USB cable | Data-enabled | 1 | Serial communication and Arduino programming |
| Power supply | Voltage/current to confirm | 1 | Supplies the electronic and mechanical system |
| LED strip | Model and voltage to confirm | TBD | Bubble illumination |
| Water pump | Model and voltage to confirm | TBD | Water circulation or displacement |
| Motor driver / switching circuit | To confirm | TBD | Controls externally powered actuators, if required |
| Jumper wires | Male-to-male / other types | TBD | Electrical connections |
| Breadboard / prototyping board | To confirm | TBD | Circuit assembly |

## Sensor Testing Components

| Component | Specification | Quantity | Function |
|---|---|---:|---|
| NTC thermistor | 100K, Beta 3950 | 2 | Experimental breathing temperature measurement |
