
# Material Exploration and Renders

This section documents the exploration of different materials, finishes, and surface treatments for the RESPIRA installation.

The renders investigate how material selection influences the visual identity, perceived weight, reflectivity, and relationship between the mechanical structure and the soap membrane.

## Design Considerations

Material exploration considered:

- Surface reflectivity and interaction with light
- Transparency and visibility of the soap membrane
- Visual contrast between structural and organic elements
- Perceived weight and material character
- Compatibility with fabrication processes

These renders represent visual design explorations and do not necessarily reflect the final materials used in fabrication.
