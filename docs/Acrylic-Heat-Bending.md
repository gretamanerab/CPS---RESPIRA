
# Acrylic Heat Bending and Structural Fabrication

## 1. Overview

This document describes the fabrication of the curved acrylic support structure used in RESPIRA.

The structure was developed to support the mechanical assembly holding the 3D printed elements, allowing the expansion of mechanism downwards without touching ground (or in this case, the table).

The fabrication process combined digital design, laser cutting, sheet-metal rolling, and thermal forming of acrylic.

A curved metal former was manufactured to provide a consistent bending surface for shaping the acrylic into the required geometry.

## 3. Digital Design and Laser Cutting

The acrylic support structure was first developed as a two-dimensional profile in Adobe Illustrator.

The geometry was prepared for laser cutting, allowing the acrylic sheet to be cut to the required dimensions before thermal forming.

### Fabrication Process

1. Develop the acrylic support geometry in Adobe Illustrator.
3. Check the dimensions and scale of the file.
4. Export or prepare the file for the laser cutter.
5. Laser-cut the acrylic profile

## 4. Fabrication of the Metal Bending Former

A cylindrical metal former was fabricated to provide a rigid surface around which the acrylic could be shaped.

The former was produced from sheet metal, approximately 14 gauge, using a sheet-metal slip roller.

### Process

1. Prepare a sheet-metal section suitable for the required bending diameter.
2. Position the metal sheet between the rollers of the slip-rolling machine.
3. Gradually pass the sheet through the rollers to introduce curvature.
4. Adjust the roller settings and repeat until the desired curvature is achieved.
5. Check the resulting cylindrical geometry against the intended acrylic structure.
6. Use the curved metal component as the bending former.

The metal former provided a physical reference for the intended curvature during the acrylic heat-bending process.

## 5. Acrylic Heat-Bending Process

Following laser cutting, the acrylic profile was thermally formed around the metal former.

The acrylic was heated using a hot plate, with the temperature checked periodically using a temperature probe.

### Recorded Heating Conditions

| Parameter | Recorded Information |
|---|---|
| Material | Acrylic (PMMA) |
| Heating equipment | Hot plate |
| Reported hot-plate setting | Approximately 170°C |
| Temperature monitoring | Periodic measurements using a temperature probe |
| Forming method | Manual bending around a metal former |
| Protective equipment | Heat-resistant gloves |

### Fabrication Steps

1. Position the metal bending former near the heating workstation.
3. Heat the acrylic using the hot plate.
4. Periodically monitor the temperature using a temperature probe.
5. Once sufficiently softened, carefully shape the acrylic around the curved metal former, use heat-resistant gloves when handling heated components.
7. Maintain the required curvature while the acrylic cools through both hands and table clamps. In this process, 4 were used, strategically positioned. 
8. Allow the material to become rigid before removing it from the former.

The bending process converted the flat laser-cut profile into a curved structural component suitable for integration into the mechanical assembly.

## 6. Integration into RESPIRA

After cooling, the formed acrylic component was incorporated into the mechanical structure.
The heat-bending method allowed a three-dimensional structural component to be fabricated from a flat sheet without requiring a dedicated injection mould or a fully machined part.

## 7. Fabrication Considerations

Several factors are important when evaluating the process.

### Bending Accuracy

The curvature of the metal former determines the approximate final shape of the acrylic.

Dimensional accuracy also depends on heating uniformity, material thickness, and springback during cooling.

### Material Integrity

The finished component should be inspected for:

- Cracking or crazing
- Surface bubbling
- Localised deformation
- Uneven curvature
- Damage around laser-cut edges

### Safety

Acrylic heating should be performed with suitable ventilation, appropriate thermal protection, and equipment approved for thermoplastic forming.

The heating temperature and method should be checked against the manufacturer's recommendations before repeating the process.
